INTRODUCING FOCUSBAND

Awareness you can
feel. Not just see.

FocusBand brings your app limits, gentle haptic nudges, and daily insights together in one simple companion experience. It's a small interruption with a bigger intention.

✓ Track time across your chosen apps
✓ Set limits that fit your day
✓ Feel a gentle nudge when time is up

SIMPLE BY DESIGN

Four steps to a more intentional day.

A small, supportive loop that puts you back in the driver's seat.

⌁01

Connect your band

Pair your FocusBand and get a quick read on its connection status.

▣02

Choose what to track

Pick the apps and websites you want to be more intentional with.

◷03

Set a daily limit

Choose a time budget that supports your routine and your priorities.

≋04

Get a gentle nudge

When time is up, a vibration and dashboard alert help you pause.

✦   Alerts are gentle by design. FocusBand never uses electric shocks.
